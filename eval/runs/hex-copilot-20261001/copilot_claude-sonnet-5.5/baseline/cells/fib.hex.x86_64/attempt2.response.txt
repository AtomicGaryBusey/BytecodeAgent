The jz ends at 12 and the target is 29, so the displacement is 17 = 0x11. The jnz ends at 29 and the target is 12, so the displacement is -17 = 0xef.

The loop is the 5 instructions from offset 12 to 28, 17 bytes in total, so jnz needs -(17+2) = -19 = 0xed. Recomputing: the loop body runs from 12 to 29, which is 17 bytes, and the jnz instruction itself is 2 bytes of that, so the end is at 29 and the displacement is 12 - 29 = -17 = 0xef. That fixes the jnz byte at `ef`, not `f3`.

Final: jz displacement 0x11, jnz displacement 0xef.
